Privacy Policy

Kasi Lingo  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Kasi Lingo mobile application (package name: com.cyriandev.kasilingo) published by Sibusiso Ndlovu ("we", "us", or "our"). Kasi Lingo is a crowd-sourced dictionary of South African township slang.

Information We Collect

To create an account, we ask for your email address, which we use to send a one-time passcode for sign-in — we never collect or store a password. You also choose a username and, optionally, a profile photo.

Content you choose to submit — words, definitions, comments, votes, audio pronunciation recordings, and the township tags you attach to a word — is stored on our servers and may be visible to other users of the app. If you record a pronunciation, the app requests microphone access to do so.

We use a randomly generated install identifier, kept only on your device, to understand how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened or searched). This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or location, and you can turn it off at any time from your profile settings — nothing is recorded while it's off.

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can send you alerts (for example, about replies or the word of the day). You can disable notifications at any time in your device or app settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising. The app does not display ads. Your data is stored with Supabase , our backend provider, which processes it on our behalf under their own privacy policy.

Your Choices

You can edit or delete your submitted content, update your profile, and delete your account at any time from within the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and personal data from our systems; content you've contributed to the shared dictionary may be retained in anonymised form to preserve the dictionary for other users.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
