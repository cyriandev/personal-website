Delete Your Account

Kasi Lingo  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Kasi Lingo account (package name: com.cyriandev.kasilingo) and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete in the app

Open Kasi Lingo, go to your Profile tab, tap Edit profile, then scroll down and tap Delete account. This takes effect immediately — no waiting period.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us at cyriandev@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done, typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile, email address, and push notification token from our systems. Words, definitions, comments, votes, and audio pronunciation recordings you've contributed to the shared dictionary may be retained in anonymised form — detached from your identity — to preserve the dictionary for other users.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and how it's used, or contact us at cyriandev@gmail.com.
